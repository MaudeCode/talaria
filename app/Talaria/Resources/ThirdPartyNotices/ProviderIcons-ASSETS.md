`ProviderIconTogetherAI` identifies Hermes' `Mixture of Agents` virtual
provider. Together AI introduced the Mixture-of-Agents name and published the
original `togethercomputer/MoA` reference implementation; Hermes runs its own
local reference-model/aggregator implementation and does not route this
provider through Together AI's API.
